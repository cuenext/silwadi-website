About Silwadi

Established care. Evolving practice.

Since 1980, Silwadi Dental Center has provided dental care in Abu Dhabi with an emphasis on continuity of care, clear communication and access to a multidisciplinary clinical team.

Since 1980More than four decades of dental care in Abu Dhabi, with general dentists and specialists working within one centre.

Our story

A centre built around continuity of care.

The centre has grown with changes in dentistry while keeping the patient journey straightforward: understand the concern, plan carefully and coordinate the right level of care.

Long-standing care in Abu Dhabi

Silwadi Dental Center has served patients in Abu Dhabi since 1980. That history is reflected in an institutional approach that values continuity, familiarity and responsible long-term dental planning.

A multidisciplinary model

General dentists and specialists work across prosthodontics, implantology, orthodontics, periodontics and endodontics. When a case needs specialist input, care can be coordinated within the same clinical team.

Modern workflows, clinical purpose

Digital tools are used where they support assessment, planning, communication or treatment delivery. Technology is part of the workflow rather than the message on its own.

How we work

Professional care without unnecessary complexity.

The centre’s role is to make clinical information understandable and help patients move through assessment, treatment planning and specialist care with clear next steps.

01

Clear communication

Findings, options and next steps are explained in a way patients can understand before treatment decisions are made.

02

Multidisciplinary planning

Cases can involve input from the appropriate general dentist or specialist when more than one discipline is relevant.

03

Continuity of care

The aim is not only to address the immediate concern but to maintain a coherent plan for ongoing oral health.

Patient journey

Care organised around the next clinical step.

02Plan

03Coordinate

04Review
