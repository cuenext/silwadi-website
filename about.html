Our story

A familiar name in Abu Dhabi dentistry.

Silwadi Dental Centre has cared for patients in Abu Dhabi since 1980. What began with Dr. Munir Silwadi continues today through a team of general dentists and specialists working together.

We keep the experience simple: listen carefully, explain your options clearly and build a treatment plan around you.
