About Silwadi

Established care. Evolving practice.

Silwadi Dental Center has cared for patients in Abu Dhabi since 1980. The centre’s approach combines comprehensive dentistry with compassionate, patient-centred care, personalized treatment and clear communication.

Since 1980More than four decades of dental care in Abu Dhabi, with general dentists and specialists working within one centre.

Our approach

Care centred on the patient.

The official Silwadi philosophy puts patient comfort, individual needs and understandable treatment planning at the centre of care.

Patient-centred care

Treatment is planned around each patient’s needs, with comfort and a compassionate clinical setting treated as priorities.

Comprehensive dentistry

The centre provides routine, cosmetic and specialist dental care across a multidisciplinary team.

Education & open communication

Patient education and open communication are part of the centre’s stated approach, with procedures and options explained before treatment decisions are made.
