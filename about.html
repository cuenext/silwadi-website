About Silwadi

Established care. Evolving practice.

Silwadi Dental Center has cared for patients in Abu Dhabi since 1980. Today, the centre brings general dentists and specialists together under one roof, with continuity of care across a multidisciplinary team.

Since 1980More than four decades of dental care in Abu Dhabi, with general dentists and specialists working within one centre.

Our story

A centre built around continuity of care.

The centre has grown with Abu Dhabi while keeping the patient experience simple: understand the problem, explain the options and involve the right clinician.

Long-standing care in Abu Dhabi

More than four decades in Abu Dhabi has created something patients notice: familiarity, continuity and a team that can look beyond a single appointment.

A multidisciplinary model

General dentists and specialists work across the main dental disciplines. When specialist input is needed, it can be coordinated within the same team.

Modern workflows, clinical purpose

Digital tools are used when they make assessment, planning or communication clearer — not simply because they are new.

Professional care without unnecessary complexity.
