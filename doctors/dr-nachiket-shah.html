Dr. Nachiket Shah · Silwadi Dental Center, Abu Dhabi

Doctor profile

Dr. Nachiket Shah

Specialist Periodontist & Implantologist

Dr. Nachiket Shah is a DOH-licensed Specialist Periodontist with implant privilege and more than 14 years of clinical experience across India and the UAE. His practice focuses on periodontal treatment, implant dentistry, tissue regeneration and selected oral surgical procedures.

SpecialtySpecialist Periodontist & Implantologist
Clinical baseAl Raha Mall, Abu Dhabi
CentreDr. Munir Silwadi Dental Centre

Clinical focus

Periodontal treatmentAssessment and surgical management of periodontal conditions, including procedures for gum recession and crown lengthening where indicated.
Implant dentistrySingle-tooth, partial and full-arch implant treatment planning within his granted implant privilege.
Bone & soft-tissue proceduresSocket preservation, localized ridge augmentation, bone grafting and connective-tissue grafting.
Laser-assisted & oral surgical careUse of Diode and Er:YAG laser techniques for selected periodontal and soft-tissue procedures, together with surgical extractions where clinically indicated.

Education & professional background

  • BDS, 2001
  • MDS in Periodontology and Implantology, 2009
  • 2nd Level University Master’s in Oral Surgery & Implantology, UniCamillus, Rome
  • MSc in Quality and Safety in Healthcare Management, Royal College of Surgeons in Ireland

Languages & approach

Dr. Nachiket communicates in English, Hindi, Gujarati and Marathi, with working Arabic. He emphasizes clear treatment education, careful surgical planning and patient comfort throughout periodontal and implant care.
