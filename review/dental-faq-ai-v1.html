What is a root canal?

Root canal treatment cleans and treats the inside of a tooth when the pulp is inflamed, infected or damaged. A dentist decides whether it is needed after an examination and any necessary X-rays.

What is a dental crown?

A crown covers and protects a tooth that needs more support than a filling alone. Suitability depends on the tooth, bite, remaining structure and treatment history.

Does tooth whitening damage teeth?

Professionally supervised whitening is planned around the condition of the teeth and gums. Temporary sensitivity can occur, and a dental examination is useful before treatment.

What is a dental implant?

A dental implant is a fixture placed in the jawbone to support a replacement tooth or prosthesis. Suitability depends on bone, gum health, medical history and the treatment area.

When should my child have their first dental visit?

A first dental visit is generally recommended around the arrival of the first tooth and no later than the first birthday.

What happens at a child’s first visit?

The visit is usually focused on helping the child feel comfortable, checking the mouth when possible, and giving parents practical advice about brushing and diet.

Should children use fluoride toothpaste?

Fluoride toothpaste helps protect enamel from tooth decay. The amount should be appropriate for the child’s age and parents should supervise brushing.

When should a child see an orthodontist?

Timing depends on growth, bite development and tooth eruption. Your dentist can advise when an orthodontic assessment would be useful.
