Appointments & visits

Before you come in.

Useful information for new and returning patients planning a visit to Silwadi.

Are you accepting new patients?

Yes. New patient appointments can be requested for both Silwadi locations. Reception can help match the appointment to the right dentist or specialist based on the reason for your visit.

How do I book a new patient appointment?

You can request an appointment through our contact page or call the branch you would like to visit. If you have pain, swelling, dental trauma or another urgent concern, mention this when booking.

Do you accept insurance?

Insurance acceptance, direct billing and covered dental benefits can vary by provider and plan. Contact reception with your insurance details before the appointment so the team can confirm the current process for your policy.

Which Silwadi branch should I visit?

Silwadi has locations at Bani Yas Tower on the Corniche and at Al Raha Mall in Abu Dhabi. Dentist availability differs by branch, so check the locations page or ask reception when booking.

02

Preventive dentistry

Keeping teeth and gums healthy.

Routine care is individualized according to your oral health, risk factors and previous dental history.

How often should I have a dental cleaning?

Many patients are reviewed and cleaned at regular intervals, but there is no single schedule that is right for everyone. Your dentist or hygienist can recommend an interval based on plaque levels, gum health, decay risk and previous treatment.

Why might I need dental X-rays?

Dental X-rays can show areas that are not visible during a clinical examination, including decay between teeth, bone levels, infections, developing teeth and other findings. The type and timing of imaging should be based on clinical need.

How often should I brush my teeth?

For most people, brushing twice a day with fluoride toothpaste is a practical routine. Technique matters as much as frequency, so your dental team can show you how to clean effectively around the gumline and any dental work.

Do I really need to floss?

Cleaning between teeth helps remove plaque from areas a toothbrush may not reach. Floss is one option; interdental brushes or other aids may be more suitable depending on the spaces between your teeth and your gum condition.

03

Children’s dentistry

Questions parents ask us.

Early visits help children become familiar with dental care while giving parents practical guidance for growing teeth.

When should my child have their first dental visit?

A first dental visit is generally recommended around the arrival of the first tooth and no later than the first birthday. Earlier assessment can also be appropriate if you notice pain, trauma, unusual eruption or another concern.

What should I expect at my child’s first visit?

The first visit is usually focused on getting to know the child, checking the mouth when possible, discussing brushing and diet, and answering the parent or caregiver’s questions. What is completed depends on the child’s age, comfort and clinical needs.

Should children use fluoride toothpaste?

Fluoride toothpaste helps protect enamel from tooth decay. The amount used should be appropriate for the child’s age and ability, and parents should supervise brushing. A pediatric dentist can advise you if your child has a higher risk of decay.

When should a child see an orthodontist?

Some children benefit from an orthodontic assessment while they still have a mix of baby and permanent teeth. The timing depends on growth, bite development, eruption and the specific concern, so your dentist can advise when a specialist review is useful.

04

Dental treatments

Understanding common procedures.

A diagnosis comes first. These short explanations describe what a treatment generally means, not whether you personally need it.

What is a root canal?

Root canal treatment is used to clean and treat the inside of a tooth when the pulp becomes inflamed, infected or damaged. The tooth is disinfected and sealed, and a suitable final restoration is planned according to how much tooth structure remains.

What is a dental crown?

A crown covers and protects a tooth that has been weakened, heavily restored, fractured or otherwise needs full-coverage restoration. Whether a crown is appropriate depends on the tooth, bite, remaining structure and treatment history.

Does tooth whitening damage teeth?

Professionally supervised whitening is planned around the condition of the teeth and gums and the whitening product being used. Temporary sensitivity can occur. A dental examination is useful before whitening, especially if you have decay, gum problems, cracks or existing restorations.

What is a dental implant?

A dental implant is a fixture placed in the jawbone to support a replacement tooth or prosthesis. Suitability depends on factors such as bone, gum health, medical history, the space being restored and the overall treatment plan.

Still have a question?

If your question is about symptoms, a previous treatment, insurance, branch availability or which dentist to see, our reception team can help arrange the appropriate next step.
