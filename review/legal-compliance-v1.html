Silwadi website review

What we should actually add.

The generic “20 things” list is useful as a prompt, but not every item belongs on Silwadi. This review separates changes we should prepare now from items that are conditional, already covered, or require an operational audit rather than another website page.

Review-only changesNo public deployment yetEnglish + Arabic legal drafts prepared
Important legal framing. Silwadi is an Abu Dhabi healthcare provider. Health information is subject to UAE healthcare-specific rules and DoH Abu Dhabi information-security requirements. The general UAE personal-data law also contains an exclusion for personal health data where sector-specific legislation regulates it. These draft pages therefore avoid pretending that a generic SaaS privacy template is sufficient.
01Privacy policyPrepare nowMissing from the public site. Bilingual review drafts are now prepared. Link it from forms and the footer after approval.
02Website termsPrepare nowUseful for medical-information boundaries, appointment confirmation, external links, intellectual property and website use. Bilingual review drafts prepared.
03Refund policyNot nowNo public online payment or deposit flow was identified. Add a payment/refund policy before taking money online.
04Cookie policyConditionalNo Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, Hotjar or Microsoft Clarity was identified in the inspected core code. The Privacy Policy can currently explain cookies/third-party embeds without creating a standalone cookie page.
05Cookie consent bannerConditionalDo not add a cosmetic banner. Reassess before introducing non-essential analytics, ad pixels, remarketing, personalization or comparable trackers.
06Form consentImprove linkThe appointment form and booking modal already require consent and warn against sensitive medical information. The next public patch should link that wording directly to the approved Privacy Policy.
07Data minimisationMaintainCurrent appointment fields are limited to contact and booking details. Keep clinical histories, diagnoses, scans and other sensitive medical data out of the general website form and AI assistant.
08Third-party servicesDocumentCurrent/future integrations include the Silwadi booking endpoint, WhatsApp, Google Maps, YouTube privacy-enhanced embeds, Instagram, Google Fonts, and—if enabled—Silwadi AI through Cloudflare/OpenAI. Maintain a vendor/data-flow register internally.
09Dark patternsMaintainNo major deceptive consent or checkout pattern was identified in the reviewed booking experience. Keep consent unticked by default and make decline/non-use straightforward.
10Hidden feesNot applicable yetThere is no online checkout. If prices, deposits or offers are introduced, display material conditions clearly before payment or booking commitment.
11Review authenticityOngoingHomepage review cards are presented as Google review highlights and link to Google Maps. Keep only traceable, accurately attributed reviews; do not fabricate or materially rewrite meaning.
12Unsupported advertising claimsHigh priorityContinue avoiding guarantees, unprovable superiority claims, misleading “best/#1” language and claims outside the evidence supplied by the clinic. Health-media advertising needs continued DoH review discipline.
13Image alt textStrong / auditCore pages inspected use alt text consistently. Empty alt text seen on decorative AI marks is appropriate. Continue checking new imagery as it is added.
14Colour contrastTestRun automated WCAG contrast checks plus manual review for text over photos, muted text, buttons, focus states and Arabic pages before launch changes.
15Keyboard navigationTestSkip links, semantic navigation and several keyboard interactions already exist. A site-wide tab-order, visible-focus, modal and carousel audit is still worthwhile.
16Business detailsStrong / verifyBranch names, phone numbers, email, addresses and opening hours are present. Keep structured data, footer and location pages synchronized whenever clinic details change.
17Children’s submissionsClarifyPediatric dentistry makes this relevant. The proposed form wording asks a parent/legal guardian to submit for someone under 18 and to keep medical details out of the general web form.
18Marketing unsubscribeConditionalAppointment confirmations are not the same as marketing. If Silwadi starts promotional email/SMS campaigns, capture marketing consent separately and provide a working opt-out/unsubscribe mechanism.
19Fonts / images / logos licensingOperational auditKeep proof of permission/licensing for clinic photography, external logos, third-party imagery, fonts and partner material. A code scan cannot prove legal ownership of each asset.
20Privacy / data requestsPrepare nowThe privacy draft provides a clear contact path for access, correction and deletion/privacy enquiries, while noting that healthcare-record obligations may limit deletion in some cases.
Recommended release sequence: approve the Privacy Policy and Terms → approve the revised appointment-consent wording → add footer legal links across English/Arabic pages → complete accessibility and asset-license checks → only then consider making the AI assistant public.
