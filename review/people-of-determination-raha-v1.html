What to expect

A clearer path from booking to care.

We keep the process straightforward and adapt the visit based on the information available and the patient’s clinical needs.

01 · Before the visit

Share what matters

Tell Al Raha reception about the dental concern and any mobility, communication, sensory or medical information relevant to the appointment.

02 · At the appointment

Assessment first

The dental team reviews the concern, medical and dental history, and any support considerations before deciding what examination or treatment is appropriate.

03 · Planning care

Discuss the next step

Findings and suitable options are discussed with the patient and, when appropriate, their family or caregiver before treatment is planned.

Dental care

Everyday dental needs, assessed individually.

Care may include preventive, restorative or gum treatment depending on the clinical assessment and the treating dentist’s scope of practice.

Examination & prevention

Assessment of teeth, gums and oral health concerns, with preventive guidance and treatment when clinically indicated.

Restorative care

Management of decay or damaged teeth when suitable after examination and treatment planning.

Gum care

Assessment and treatment planning for plaque, inflammation and periodontal concerns.

Pain & specialist referral

Assessment of urgent dental concerns and referral to an appropriate specialist when required.

Treatment suitability is assessed individually. Some patients may need adaptations, staged visits, additional medical information or specialist input before treatment proceeds.

Patient & caregiver questions

Before your appointment.

Can I tell the clinic about accessibility needs before the visit?

Yes. When requesting the appointment, tell Al Raha reception about relevant mobility, communication, sensory or other support considerations so the information can be shared with the clinical team.

Does the Al Raha branch have an accessible treatment room?

Yes. The Al Raha location includes an accessible dental treatment room. If you have a specific mobility requirement, contact reception before visiting so the team can discuss the appointment setup with you.

Can a caregiver or family member be involved?

Where appropriate, a caregiver or family member can help share medical history, communication preferences and support needs. The clinical team will explain what is suitable for the individual appointment.

What information should I share when booking?

Share the main dental concern, relevant medical information, current medications when applicable, and any mobility, communication, sensory or behavioral considerations that may affect the visit.

Tell us how we can support the visit.

Contact our Al Raha Mall reception team to request an appointment and share any access or support considerations in advance.
