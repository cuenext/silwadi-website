Official Partnership with Zayed Authority for People of Determination

Care designed around the individual

Thoughtful support, without making the visit feel complicated.

Practical needs can be shared before the appointment so the Al Raha team can prepare the environment, communication and clinical approach appropriately.

Accessible treatment environment

A dedicated treatment environment at Al Raha supports patients who may need additional space or mobility accommodation.

Individual care planning

Relevant medical, mobility and support information can be shared before the visit and considered by the clinical team.

Communication & sensory support

Tell reception about communication preferences, sensory considerations or routines that may help make the appointment clearer.

Family & caregiver collaboration

Where appropriate, a family member or caregiver can help communicate history, preferences and practical support needs.

Designed for broader support needs

A clinical setting built with access in mind.

The official Zayed Authority announcement describes the center’s treatment environment as designed to accommodate People of Determination across different support needs.

✓Environment designed to accommodate wheelchair users.
✓Designed to support individuals who rely on medical support devices, including oxygen machines and feeding tubes.
✓Staff training under the partnership includes communication and inclusive healthcare support.

These points reflect the Zayed Authority announcement of 10 April 2026. Individual appointment suitability and clinical requirements are assessed by the treating team. Read the official source.
