Privacy Policy

This draft explains how the Silwadi website handles information submitted online. It is written for the current appointment website and the proposed Silwadi AI feature, and should be legally reviewed before it is published.

Review status. This page is not yet the public Silwadi Privacy Policy. It deliberately avoids claiming a fixed retention period, a specific hosting jurisdiction, or a level of compliance that has not yet been operationally verified.

1. Who this policy covers

This website supports the services of Dr. Munir Silwadi Dental Centre at Bani Yas Tower (Corniche) and Dr. Mohamed Munir Dental Centre – LLC at Al Raha Mall in Abu Dhabi. In this draft, “Silwadi”, “we”, “us” and “our” refer to the relevant Silwadi dental centre responsible for the website interaction or appointment request.

This policy concerns information handled through the public website. Clinical records created or maintained as part of dental care are subject to separate healthcare-record, confidentiality, retention and information-security requirements.

2. Information we collect through the website

Depending on how you use the website, we may receive:

  • contact details such as your name, mobile number and email address;
  • appointment-request details such as the requested treatment category, preferred clinic, date and time;
  • optional text you enter in a notes field;
  • questions entered into Silwadi AI if that feature is enabled; and
  • basic technical information generated when a website is accessed, such as device/browser information, IP address, security logs and request metadata, where processed by the website’s hosting or security providers.

3. Why we use website information

We use website information to respond to appointment enquiries, confirm availability, route requests to the appropriate clinic team, respond to general questions, maintain website security, troubleshoot technical issues, and meet applicable legal or regulatory obligations.

We do not treat a website appointment request as a confirmed appointment until reception confirms it with you.

4. Please do not submit sensitive medical information

Important: the general appointment form and Silwadi AI are not intended for diagnoses, medical histories, radiographs, prescriptions, laboratory results or other sensitive health information. Please use the clinic’s approved clinical channels when the treating team needs medical information.

If information becomes part of your clinical care or health record, different healthcare-specific confidentiality, security and retention requirements may apply. A request to delete website information does not necessarily require deletion of a healthcare record that the clinic must retain under applicable rules.

5. Appointment requests for children

If you are requesting an appointment for a person under 18, the website submission should be made by a parent or legal guardian. Please provide only the contact and appointment details needed to arrange the visit and avoid placing the child’s medical history or other sensitive health details in the free-text field.

6. Services and providers connected to the website

Website information may be handled by providers that help us operate the site or provide a feature. Depending on the page used, these may include:

  • Silwadi booking services used to send appointment requests to the appointments team;
  • WhatsApp / Meta when you choose to contact Silwadi through WhatsApp;
  • Google Maps, YouTube and Google Fonts where maps, videos or fonts are loaded on a page;
  • Instagram when you choose to follow an external social link; and
  • Cloudflare and OpenAI if the proposed Silwadi AI assistant is enabled. In that case, the text of a question may be sent through a server-side service to an AI provider to generate a response. Users are instructed not to enter names, contact details or sensitive health information into the AI assistant.

External services operate under their own terms and privacy practices. Some providers may process technical data in jurisdictions outside the UAE. Silwadi should review vendor settings and contractual safeguards before enabling any service that will process patient-related data.

7. Cookies, analytics and embedded content

At the date of this review, the core website code inspected did not identify Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, Hotjar or Microsoft Clarity. Silwadi should not add a consent banner merely for appearance.

Some third-party content—such as an embedded map or video—may still cause the relevant provider to receive technical information when that content loads or when you interact with it. If Silwadi later introduces non-essential analytics, advertising, remarketing or comparable tracking technologies, the cookie/privacy disclosure and consent configuration should be reviewed before deployment.

8. Retention and security

Website information should be retained only for as long as reasonably needed for the purpose for which it was collected, operational needs, security, and applicable legal or regulatory obligations. Silwadi should maintain a documented retention schedule rather than placing an unverified number of months or years on this page.

Silwadi uses technical and organisational safeguards intended to reduce unauthorised access, alteration, loss or disclosure. No website or electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Access, correction and deletion/privacy enquiries

You may contact Silwadi to ask about personal information you submitted through the website, request correction of inaccurate details, or request deletion where appropriate. Some information may need to be retained for legal, regulatory, security or healthcare-record purposes.

To make a privacy/data request, email info@silwadidentalcentres.ae with the subject “Privacy / Data Request”. Please do not email sensitive medical information simply to identify yourself; the clinic may need to verify your identity through a safer process before acting on a request.
