1. Purpose of this website

The Silwadi website provides general information about the dental centres, clinicians, services, locations, opening hours and ways to request an appointment. Website content is informational and may change as clinic schedules, services and operational details change.

2. Website information is not a diagnosis

General dental information on this website does not replace an examination, diagnosis or treatment plan from a licensed clinician who has assessed the individual patient. Do not delay urgent or emergency care because of something read on the website.

Treatment suitability, expected outcomes, risks and alternatives depend on an individual clinical assessment.

3. Appointment requests, prices and insurance

Submitting a form or sending a WhatsApp message is a request for contact and does not itself confirm an appointment. Reception will confirm availability.

Where the website refers to fees, insurance, promotions or treatment availability, final details should be confirmed directly with the clinic. Insurance coverage and patient benefits depend on the applicable plan and insurer requirements.

4. Silwadi AI

If Silwadi AI is made publicly available, it is intended for general clinic and dental-information guidance. It is not a dentist, does not diagnose, and should not be used for emergencies or for treatment decisions.

Do not enter names, phone numbers, email addresses, medical histories, diagnoses, test results, images or other sensitive health information into the AI assistant. More detail is provided in the Privacy Policy review.

5. External links and services

The website may link to or embed services operated by third parties, such as WhatsApp, Google Maps, YouTube, Instagram or other providers. Those services are controlled by their respective operators and may have their own terms and privacy practices.

6. Website content and intellectual property

Unless otherwise stated, the Silwadi name, website design, original text, clinic photographs and other original website materials are intended for Silwadi’s website and may not be copied, republished or used commercially without appropriate permission. Third-party names, trademarks, logos and materials remain the property of their respective owners.

7. Acceptable use

You must not deliberately misuse the website, attempt unauthorised access, interfere with its operation, submit malicious code, impersonate another person, or use appointment and contact features for abusive, fraudulent or unlawful activity.

8. Website availability and accuracy

Silwadi aims to keep public clinic information accurate and the website available, but temporary errors, maintenance or changes can occur. For time-sensitive details such as opening hours, clinician availability, fees or insurance, contact reception for confirmation.

9. Applicable law

These website terms are intended to operate under the applicable laws and regulations of the United Arab Emirates and the Emirate of Abu Dhabi. Nothing in these terms is intended to exclude rights or obligations that cannot lawfully be excluded.

10. Contact

Questions about the website can be sent to info@silwadidentalcentres.ae. For appointment matters, contact the Bani Yas Tower (Corniche) branch on +971 2 626 2042 or Al Raha Mall on +971 2 666 2408.
