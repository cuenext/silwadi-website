What a general dentist can help with

General dentistry covers routine examination, diagnosis, preventive care and common restorative needs. It is often the right starting point when you have a concern but do not yet know whether specialist care is needed.

Routine and restorative care

Depending on clinical findings, care may include examinations, fillings, treatment of damaged teeth, preventive advice and hygiene support. More complex problems can be referred to the relevant specialist within the team.
