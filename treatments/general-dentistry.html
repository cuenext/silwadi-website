When to consider care

Reasons to see a general dentist

You do not need to know the name of the treatment before booking. Start with the concern and the dentist can assess the cause.

01

Routine check-up

Regular review can identify oral health changes before they become more difficult to manage.

02

Sensitivity

Cold, sweet or biting sensitivity should be assessed to understand the cause.

03

Possible cavity

A dark spot, food trapping or pain may require clinical examination and imaging.

04

Broken or chipped tooth

Damaged teeth can be assessed for repair, protection or specialist referral.

05

Bleeding gums

Persistent bleeding can be associated with gum inflammation and should be evaluated.

These examples are general information only. A dentist or specialist needs to assess your individual situation before recommending treatment.

Treatment

What general dentistry can include

Treatment depends on the diagnosis. General dental care covers a broad range of routine and restorative needs.

01

Dental examinations

Assessment of teeth, gums, existing dental work and the concern that brought you in.

02

Fillings & restorations

Conservative repair of teeth affected by decay, wear or minor fracture when appropriate.

03

Preventive care

Oral-hygiene guidance, professional cleaning and prevention planning based on individual risk.

04

Specialist referral

Complex endodontic, orthodontic, periodontal or prosthodontic concerns can be referred within the team.

Your care pathway

A simple care pathway

The appointment starts with diagnosis rather than assuming which treatment you need.

01

Listen

Understand the concern, symptoms and relevant history.

02

Examine

Assess the teeth, gums and supporting structures.

03

Explain

Discuss the findings and appropriate options.

04

Treat or refer

Provide general care or involve a specialist when indicated.

FAQ

General dentistry: common questions

Short answers to common questions. Your dentist can explain how the information applies to your own case.

How often should I have a dental check-up?
The right interval depends on your oral health and risk factors. Your dentist can recommend a review schedule after examining you.
Can a general dentist refer me to a specialist?
Yes. If the problem needs specialist care, the dentist can direct you to the appropriate discipline within the team.
Do you provide preventive dental care?
Yes. Preventive planning and oral-hygiene support are part of general dental care, based on individual needs.
Do I need an X-ray at every visit?
No. Dental imaging is selected according to clinical need, previous records and the condition being assessed.
Can I book even if I do not know what treatment I need?
Yes. Tell reception what is bothering you. A general dentist can assess the problem and explain the appropriate next step.

Not sure which dentist you need?

Start with a general dental assessment and our team can guide the next step.
