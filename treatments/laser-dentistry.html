FAQ

Common questions

Does laser dentistry replace the drill or all surgery?
No. It is a tool for specific indications, not a replacement for every conventional technique.
Is laser treatment always painless?
No. Comfort varies by procedure and patient; anaesthesia may still be needed.
Can I request laser treatment for any dental problem?
You can ask about it, but the dentist must first decide whether it is clinically suitable.
