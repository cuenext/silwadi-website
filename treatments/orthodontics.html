What orthodontic treatment addresses

Orthodontic care focuses on tooth alignment and the way the upper and lower teeth meet. A specialist assessment helps determine whether treatment is needed and which approach fits the case.

Braces or clear aligners?

The right appliance depends on the bite, tooth position, age and treatment goals. Options may include fixed braces, clear aligners such as Invisalign in selected cases, and retainers after active treatment. Not every option is suitable for every patient.
