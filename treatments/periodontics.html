Care for the gums

Periodontal Care in Abu Dhabi

Healthy gums support your teeth. Bleeding, swelling or changes around a tooth deserve an assessment so the cause and the right level of care are clear.

When to ask

Reasons to seek an assessment

The examples below help you decide when to speak with a dentist. They are not a diagnosis.

01

Bleeding or swollen gums

Bleeding that keeps returning is a reason to have the gums checked.

02

Persistent bad breath

Several causes are possible, including inflammation around teeth.

03

Loose teeth or receding gums

Changes in support or gum position should be assessed rather than assumed to be normal.

A clinician needs to examine you before recommending treatment.

Care options

What care may include

Your plan depends on the diagnosis, health history and what matters to you.

01

Periodontal examination

The clinician assesses gum health, deposits, pockets and supporting structures.

02

Non-surgical care

Professional cleaning and root surface treatment may be recommended according to the diagnosis.

03

Surgical options

Selected cases may need specialist procedures after initial assessment and discussion.

04

Supportive maintenance

Follow-up and home-care guidance help manage risk over time.

Your visit

From assessment to follow-up

01

Diagnose

02

Treat

03

Reassess

Review healing and set a maintenance schedule suited to your risk.

FAQ

Common questions

Is bleeding when brushing normal?
Occasional bleeding can have several causes. If it persists, a dental assessment is appropriate.
Will cleaning alone treat every gum problem?
No. Treatment depends on the diagnosis and how much the supporting tissues are affected.
Can lost gum support grow back?
Some situations may have regenerative options, but suitability depends on the defect and specialist assessment.

Ready to discuss your options?

Tell reception what is concerning you. The team can guide your appointment request.
