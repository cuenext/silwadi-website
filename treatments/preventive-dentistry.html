Care options

What care may include

Your plan depends on the diagnosis, health history and what matters to you.

01

Examination and assessment

Review the teeth, gums, existing work and concerns you have noticed.

02

Professional hygiene care

Cleaning and personalized plaque-control advice may be recommended when indicated.

03

Risk-based prevention

Fluoride or sealants may be considered for suitable patients after clinical review.

04

Follow-up planning

A recall interval is agreed according to your needs, not a fixed rule for everyone.

Your visit

From assessment to follow-up

01

Review

Discuss symptoms, habits, medical history and previous dental care.

02

Examine

Assess teeth and gums; use imaging only when clinically indicated.

03

Plan

Agree on home-care steps, treatment needs and an appropriate follow-up interval.

FAQ

Common questions

How often should I visit the dentist?
It depends on your oral health and risk factors. Your dentist can recommend an interval after examining you.
Does a check-up always include an X-ray?
No. Imaging is selected according to clinical need and previous records.
Is cleaning the same as treating gum disease?
Not always. Periodontal disease needs a diagnosis and may require additional specialist treatment.

Ready to discuss your options?

Tell reception what is concerning you. The team can guide your appointment request.
