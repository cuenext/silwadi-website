When to ask

Reasons to seek an assessment

The examples below help you decide when to speak with a dentist. They are not a diagnosis.

01

Missing teeth

A gap can affect chewing, neighbouring teeth and the way the bite feels.

02

Worn or damaged teeth

Heavily worn or fractured teeth may need more than a simple filling.

03

Older dental work

A crown, bridge or denture that no longer fits comfortably should be reviewed.

A clinician needs to examine you before recommending treatment.

Care options

What care may include

Your plan depends on the diagnosis, health history and what matters to you.

01

Crowns and bridges

Restorations may protect a suitable tooth or replace a missing tooth after assessment.

02

Removable dentures

An option for replacing several teeth when appropriate for the mouth and preferences.

03

Implant-supported restorations

A crown, bridge or denture can be planned around implants when suitability is confirmed.

04

Full-mouth rehabilitation

For complex wear or multiple missing teeth, treatment can be phased around function and maintainability.

Your visit

From assessment to follow-up

01

Assess

Examine the teeth, gums, bite and existing restorations; use imaging when indicated.

02

Plan

Discuss realistic options, sequence, maintenance and alternatives.

03

Restore

Carry out the agreed treatment and review comfort, fit and function.

FAQ

Common questions

Is a crown always needed for a damaged tooth?
No. The choice depends on the remaining tooth structure, the bite and the clinical findings.
Are implants the only way to replace missing teeth?
No. Bridges and removable dentures may also be options. The dentist will explain suitability.
How long will treatment take?
It varies with the number of teeth and the selected plan. Your dentist can outline the stages after assessment.

Ready to discuss your options?

Tell reception what is concerning you. The team can guide your appointment request.
